# Sony FE batch 2

Fifteen native Projects represent numerical examples from two Sony-assigned patent publications.
The batch contains examples 1–5 of US20190004278A1 and examples 1–4 and 6–11 of US9491348B2.
[US20190004278A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20190004278A1/en) · [US9491348B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9491348B2/en)

The records label nominal source image heights and circle diameters as estimates from the printed
focal length and half field angle. Usable Project image-circle coverage remains unconfirmed.

Each Project represents infinity focus. Materials use constant refractive indices at the reference
wavelength. The models use a uniform 13 mm clear semi-diameter, or 11 mm for US9491348B2 example 6
to avoid an intersection between neighbouring surfaces. The stop is marked `STOP_NOT_BLOCKING`,
so stop-dependent constraints remain unconfirmed.

For US9491348B2 example 11, the Project uses a 0.001 mm gap after surface 13 where the source prints
zero. The record documents the substitution under `ZERO_GAP_SUBSTITUTED`.
